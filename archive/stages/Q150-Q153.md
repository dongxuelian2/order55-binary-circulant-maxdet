# Q=150–168 tail stage

`verify_q150_boundary.py` places every Q=150 color allocation below the former
Q=153 trace envelope.  The proved color congruence leaves exactly four genuine
post-Q150 shells before trace stability takes over:

- `Q=153`
- `Q=159`
- `Q=162`
- `Q=168`

`verify_tail_shells.py` first certifies these four shells individually and
records their decreasing stationary trace envelopes.  Their approximate ratios
to the published record are

`1.0354203691, 1.0214560586, 1.0145881916, 1.0010737523`.

`verify_tail_structural_reduction.py` then closes Q=162 and Q=168 and reduces
the other two shells to nine explicit branches.  It uses exact sparse
abstract-Gram enumeration, rational Sylvester spectral brackets, two-hub Schur
residuals, exact internal determinant maxima, and weighted Motzkin--Straus
spectral caps.

`verify_tail_final_closure.py` finishes those nine branches.  A Schur--Cauchy
inequality closes all five residual size-13 allocations.  Q=159 type `(14,1)`
is treated componentwise; its only connected boundary is the 14-vertex tree
case at internal energy 117.  Exact matching-polynomial enumeration of all
3,159 unlabeled trees finds the unique maximum at `P14`, still below the
record.  The final Q=153 all-same branch is split by the number of support
isolates: clique-five spectral control handles the low-isolate range,
`AH=HB` forces enough 15-eigenvalues in the high-isolate range, and the three
intermediate K6 boundary supports are closed by Fischer/Schur bounds.

Consequently the complete tail theorem is now

`strict counterexample => Q <= 150`.

Q>=171 was already below the record by `verify_trace_stability.py`, so every
genuine shell Q=153,159,162,168 is eliminated.  Exact maximality is not yet
proved because the current Q=150 certificate is a boundary comparison against
the old Q=153 envelope rather than a direct record-level exclusion.  The next
stage should therefore attack Q=150 directly.
