# Q=150–168 tail stage

`verify_q150_boundary.py` closes the Q=150 color allocations below the Q=153
trace envelope.  The proved color congruence leaves exactly four genuine tail
shells before trace stability takes over:

- `Q=153`
- `Q=159`
- `Q=162`
- `Q=168`

`verify_tail_shells.py` now certifies these four shells individually.  For each
shell it evaluates every two-level stationary multiplicity `m=1,...,14`,
proves that the maximum is attained at `m=1`, records the exact rational trace
envelope, and applies the `3^14` divisibility and rational Eisenstein-norm
sieve.  The four envelopes decrease strictly with Q.  Their approximate ratios
to the published record are respectively

`1.0354203691, 1.0214560586, 1.0145881916, 1.0010737523`.

Thus Q=153 remains the unique tail shell controlling the global analytic upper
bound.  Q=168 is already within about `0.1074%` of the record, while Q=171 is
strictly below the record by `verify_trace_stability.py`.  The shellwise
arithmetic sieve does not yet eliminate any of Q=153,159,162,168 completely,
so exact maximality remains open and further progress must use structural
constraints inside these four shells rather than a coarser trace estimate.

`verify_aggregate_upper.py` consumes this genuine-shell certificate directly;
it no longer pads the tail with the impossible Q=156 and Q=165 values.
